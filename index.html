About Us

Eliz Community Centre (ECC) was founded by Nandawula Betty, a single mother to three boys, committed to helping vulnerable and abandoned children.

We currently care for some children. While some parents contribute small fees, 7 children are fully supported through food, care, and education assistance, and others are supported in simple things such as scholastic materials, food during the holidays, free skills such as singing, musical instruments, computer skills.

We currently want to grow as a community and be able to raise more children
